# Resource and Role Disclosure Worksheet

Answer each item with a factual `yes`, `no`, or concise description. Do not infer
an answer from an affiliation alone.

1. Did any author hold a paid employment, consultancy, ownership, or leadership
   role with an organization connected to this study while it was conducted?
2. Did any organization own, lease, administer, or pay for a measurement device,
   computing resource, software licence, model checkpoint, storage, or data used
   in the study? List each relevant resource.
3. Did any non-author employee, manager, legal office, product team, or other
   representative influence study design, data collection, analysis,
   interpretation, manuscript preparation, or the decision to submit?
4. Does any organization claim ownership, licence rights, approval rights, or
   publication-review rights over the code, data, models, or manuscript?
5. Were any internal, non-public organizational data, prompts, or operational
   traces used? If yes, describe access restrictions and exclude them from a
   public artifact unless their disclosure is authorized.

Draft the final competing-interest statement only from confirmed answers. Amend
any statement that asserts no organizational role when an answer indicates a
relevant role or resource contribution.
